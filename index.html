Karla Angela Sison / About Me

Karla Angela
Sison.

A detail-oriented interior design graduate with a passion for creating functional and aesthetically pleasing spaces.

Skilled in conceptualizing design ideas and bringing them to life through design software, with an eagerness to keep learning and growing in interior design.

Her work explores residential interiors, hospitality spaces, and short-term rentals — balancing functionality, aesthetics, and each client’s vision.
